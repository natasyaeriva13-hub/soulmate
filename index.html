01 — WORDS I MEAN

My Dearest May

𓏲ּ 𝐓𝐎 𝐌𝐘 𝐌𝐀𝐘, 𝐌𝐘 𝐍𝐄𝐖 𝐅𝐀𝐕𝐎𝐑𝐈𝐓𝐄 𝐏𝐄𝐑𝐒𝐎𝐍. ♡

Dear, May.

Perhaps this is just the beginning of our story, but somehow, I'm already looking forward to all the little things we'll get to experience together. The random conversations, the unexpected laughter, the silly arguments, the moments of missing each other, and even the ordinary days that might become special simply because I get to share them with you.

It's a little funny how someone can slowly become a part of your thoughts without even realizing it. And now, here you are, someone I get to call mine. Someone I want to know a little more every day, someone whose happiness I want to be a part of, and someone I hope will find a little comfort in having me around.

May, I know we're still at the very beginning. We haven't learned every little detail about each other, and we probably still have so many things to understand. But I don't want us to be in a hurry. I'd rather take my time learning how you think, what makes you smile, what hurts you, and what makes you feel loved.

I want to know the version of you that the world doesn't always get to see. The quiet you, the tired you, the overthinking you, and the you that sometimes needs reassurance without knowing how to ask for it.

I want you to feel that you don't always have to be okay around me. You don't have to hide your feelings just to keep things peaceful. If something bothers you, let's talk about it. If we misunderstand each other, let's learn to listen. And if life ever gets a little difficult, I hope we remember that we're on the same side.

I'm not here to promise you a perfect love story, May. I'm here because I want to give this story a genuine chance. I want us to grow, to learn, and to become better partners without losing ourselves along the way.

And honestly? I think there's something beautiful about new beginnings. The fact that we still have our firsts waiting for us, the memories we haven't made, the places we haven't visited together, and all the little moments that will someday become stories we tell each other again and again.

If I could wish for one thing for us, it would be that we never stop being curious about each other. That even after knowing one another for a long time, we still ask how our day went, still care about the smallest details, and still choose to make each other feel appreciated.

I don't need us to have everything figured out right now. I just hope we can keep choosing honesty, kindness, and each other as we discover what love means to us.

Thank you for being here, May. Thank you for becoming a part of this new chapter of my life. I don't know exactly where this story will take us, but I'm genuinely happy that I get to begin it with you.

So, here's to us. To this little beginning, to the affection we're learning to give, and to the possibility of building something beautiful together. And if someday you happen to read this letter again, I hope you'll remember that once, at the very beginning of our story, there was someone who was genuinely happy to have met you..

Someone who was excited about the little things. Someone who wanted to know you beyond the surface.

Someone who hoped to make you feel loved, not only through words, but through the way they treated you.

And that someone is writing this letter for you now.. It's a new chapter, May. And I'm glad you're in it. ♡

With love,
*Someone who's happy to call you theirs.* 𐙚
